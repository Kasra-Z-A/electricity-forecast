04 / Method

How forecasts are calculated

Official forecasts are made by the published model. The model behind each day is named under its chart, and section 03 compares all tracked models on the same days.

Official inputs freeze at 10:30 in Vienna on the preceding day. Publication is due by 10:45. Daylight-saving changes are handled explicitly.

06 / MLOps loop

How a change reaches production

Every improvement travels the same circuit. A change is planned from measured error, tested, built once into a pinned image, deployed to the forecasting host, and scored again once it runs. The measurement is what closes the loop and decides the next change.
